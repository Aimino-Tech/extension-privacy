# Aimino Invoice Collector BETA — Privacy Policy

**Effective:** 25 November 2026  
**Applies to:** version 0.1.1, the local-only invoice review extension.

**Publisher and contact:** Aimino Tech GmbH, Germany; info@aimino.de; https://aimino.de/.

The extension processes the company/account label, invoice checklist references and original PDF or XML files that you explicitly choose in the open extension page. These may contain personal identification information (such as names, postal addresses or tax IDs) and financial/payment information (such as invoice amounts, bank details and payment references). The purpose is to assemble a local invoice-review batch and compute SHA-256 hashes of the selected originals.

The extension does not obtain information from other websites, collect browsing history, require login, upload files, send information to Aimino or third parties, or use analytics, advertising or remote code. Processing occurs locally in the browser. The working data is not written to extension storage; closing or reloading the page clears the working session. Exported ZIP files are saved on your device through your browser and remain there until you delete them. Your browser and operating system may maintain their own download history and files. Share exported files only with the intended authorized recipient. You can stop processing by closing the extension page and remove the extension through Chrome.

Aimino does not sell or transfer data processed by this version. The use of information received from Chrome APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. If you contact support, the email information you choose to provide is handled separately to respond to your request; please do not send confidential invoices or credentials. Contact info@aimino.de for privacy inquiries and requests. This policy does not cover future connected or automated editions; a new policy and disclosures must be reviewed before those features are released.
